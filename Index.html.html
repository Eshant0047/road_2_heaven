How it works

One route, four steps, a maintenance list that writes itself

1

Capture

A camera mounted on the bus records the road surface during its normal, everyday route.

2

Detect

An AI model scans each frame and flags potholes, cracks, and faded markings as it goes.

3

Geotag

Every flagged frame is stamped with the bus's location, so the defect has an exact address.

4

Dispatch

The municipal team gets a ranked, mapped list — worst defects first, no site visit needed to find them.
